How a project runs

  1. Meet & discussYour idea, goals, users and constraints.
  2. DesignFeasibility, architecture, hardware and software choices.
  3. PrototypeA working demo early, to test the idea on real hardware and data.
  4. Build & testFull implementation from scratch, with your feedback along the way.
  5. Hand overSource code, documentation and demo, plus report support and training.

Good fit for

  • Students and researchers with a thesis, research or competition project
  • Lecturers and makers who want to test a novel idea
  • Anyone with an original IoT / AIoT / AI idea who wants it built, documented and explained

Note: I work with individuals only. I do not take on projects on behalf of companies or organizations.
